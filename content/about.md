## What is InterviewPrep?

InterviewPrep is a free, no-fluff technical reference for software developers preparing for coding interviews. Every article is written to be concise, practical, and interview-focused — covering the concepts that actually come up in technical rounds at companies big and small.

## What you'll find here

The site is organised around three core topics that interviewers return to again and again:

- **C++ Fundamentals & Modern C++** — memory management, smart pointers, move semantics, RAII, templates, and the STL. If you work in C++ or are interviewing at a company that does (embedded, gaming, fintech, systems), this is your starting point.
- **Data Structures & Algorithms** — arrays, linked lists, trees, graphs, sorting, searching, and dynamic programming. The essential patterns for cracking coding rounds, coming soon.
- **System Design** — scalability, databases, caching, message queues, and microservices. For senior roles where the architecture round is the differentiator, coming soon.

## Who is this for?

Primarily mid-level developers — engineers with a few years of experience who are preparing to move up or switch companies. The content assumes you can already write code; it focuses on depth and interview-specific insights rather than beginner tutorials.

## How to use the site

Use the navigation menu to jump straight to a topic. Each page is self-contained — you can read it top to bottom in one sitting, or use it as a quick reference when you need to brush up on a specific concept before an interview. All content is free, no account required.
